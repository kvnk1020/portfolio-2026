# Design Brief

## Website goals

- Impress design and technology leaders. Get them to think "I should hire Kevin."
- Make me a prime candidate to get hired by OpenAI, Anthropic, Docusign, Whatnot, Ramp, or Stripe, as a mid to senior level Product Designer.

## Positioning statement

Kevin brings architectural systems-thinking to complex enterprise security and data products — turning dense, high-stakes problems into interfaces people actually trust.

*(Shorter hero-headline variant, if needed: "Systems-minded product design for high-stakes enterprise software.")*

## Overall guidance

- I want to start by seeding this site with the content I already have, however it will evolve over time.
- v1 will be a migration of my current website's structure and layout. I'll provide screenshots.
- Refer to `sitemap.md` for the full pages and sections list.
- I want this to be easy to iterate on.
- I want visitors to easily grasp what product designer I am and remember me in 30 seconds.
- I'm open to changing anything that would better serve the stated goals above.
- I still want a good amount of flexibility in terms of designing custom views, styles, and interactions within that migrated structure, so I can create a standout site.
- I'm thinking of this as professional portfolio site first, public knowledge base second.

## Things to highlight

- **My expertise:** Adaptable designer with experience in cybersecurity and enterprise data management. Attention to detail and big-picture thinking from a product perspective.
- **My unique background:** Pivoted from Architecture to UX Design. I design with systematic thinking.

## Design tone

- Should use responsive design best practices. Must look and feel great on both mobile and desktop.
- Minimal, subtle, intentional — but with a high level of detail on craft and interaction.
- Light mode default with a dark mode switch.
- Subtle interactions and transitions between pages and navigation.
- Example shadcn preset (for component styling tokens, not layout/structure): https://ui.shadcn.com/create?preset=b27Hm7nM&item=preview
