## Project Summary

**Problem — Difficulty navigating between documents and scan results**  
Data scientists struggled to navigate between the main work area and scan results. They couldn't tell if scan results were outdated, found the results hard to read, didn't know which issue to fix first, and had trouble locating the flagged code.

**Action — Applied severity grouping and status clarity**  
I organized user feedback and refined one direction with the senior designer and engineering team: severity-level grouping to signal fix priority, plus relative timestamps and status notifications so users could tell at a glance if their notebook had changed since the last scan.

**Result — Faster editing, stronger adoption, and funding impact**
The redesign reduced the total code editing process time by an average of 20%. Beyond product metrics, the launch contributed to a 33% increase in the company's LinkedIn followers in a month and supported the company's $35M Series A raise.
