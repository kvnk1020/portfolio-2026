## Project Summary

**Problem — Complex upfront configurations**
The phishing campaign builder was too complex and time consuming, requiring too many upfront configurations. This created high cognitive load and friction for 80% of cybersecurity managers, slowing campaign creation workflow.

**Action — Pre-configured settings into presets**
I led the design initiative to repackage complex configurations into preset-based attack selections and simplified the attack preview experience. Partnered closely with Product and Engineering to validate usability, align with business goals, and ensure compatibility with the existing back-end architecture.

**Result — Reduced setup time and back-end load**
Reduced managers' campaign setup time by 60% through preset-based workflows, while the redesigned preview experience decreased unnecessary back-end load and improved system efficiency.
